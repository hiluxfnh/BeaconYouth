    About BYC — standard copy

    Use this approved text in articles, press releases, and event descriptions. Please do not modify it.

    Beacon Youth Collective (BYC) is a youth-led, evidence-driven nonprofit organization based in Bertoua, Cameroon. Founded in 2025, BYC empowers young people through four core programs: Education, Climate Action, Tech for Good, and Gender Equality & GBV Prevention. BYC co-designs its programs with schools, local councils, and partner organizations to ensure community relevance and sustained impact, and has been represented at international forums including the United Nations Commission on the Status of Women (CSW) and COP climate negotiations. For more information, contact beaconyouthcollective@gmail.com.
