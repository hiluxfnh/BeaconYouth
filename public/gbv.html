    Gender Equality & Ending GBV

    We prevent and respond to violence against women and girls through awareness, allyship with men & boys, SRHR education, and survivor-centered referral pathways.

    Education Climate Action Tech for Good Gender & GBV

    Prevention & Allyship

    Evidence-based curricula for schools and communities — engaging boys and men as active allies, addressing harmful norms respectfully but firmly.

    SRHR Education

    Age-appropriate adolescent sexual & reproductive health and rights sessions — adapted from humanitarian toolkits and delivered by trained facilitators.

    Referral Mapping

    Clear, survivor-centered pathways to trusted local services — with strict confidentiality, do-no-harm principles, and psychosocial support.

    Safeguarding & PSEAH Commitment. Safeguarding, anti-harassment, and PSEAH (Protection from Sexual Exploitation, Abuse and Harassment) policies apply to all BYC staff, volunteers, and partners. All facilitators are trained in do-no-harm principles and survivor-centred approaches before engaging with communities.
