    School Climate Clubs

    Interactive, youth-led clubs tackling waste, water, and local adaptation — guided by peer mentors using practical, hands-on approaches.

    Community Adaptation

    Practical demonstrations on rainwater harvesting, soil restoration, and nature-based solutions for vulnerable groups.

    Policy Engagement

    Youth briefs, consultations, and participation in UNFCCC/YOUNGO processes — turning community data into policy recommendations.

    What this looks like in practice

    We adapt the EcoCityAdapt platform into school-friendly modules covering climate basics, risk mapping, and practical solutions for Cameroonian schools.

    • Waste, water & tree-planting club projects
    • Youth-made community climate risk maps
    • School-ready climate literacy modules & teacher guides
    • Youth climate policy briefs and COP/YOUNGO representation
