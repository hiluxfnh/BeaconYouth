    Our Transparency Commitment

    We believe donors deserve to know exactly where their money goes. That's why we:

    • Publish annual summaries of all contributions and results
    • Break down spending by program in every report
    • Maintain zero-overhead policy for small donations
    • Respond to donor inquiries within 5 business days
